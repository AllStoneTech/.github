# All Stone Tech
### Institutional Technology Consultancy | Atlanta Metro, GA

We architect, build, and stabilize software systems for startups, 
enterprises, and small businesses that need senior technical leadership 
without the overhead of a full-time hire.

All Stone Tech is not a staffing firm. We are a principal-led consultancy — 
every engagement is owned by a senior architect who is accountable for 
outcomes, not just hours.

---

## 🛠️ What We Do
- **Fractional CTO & Technical Advisory** — strategic technical leadership 
  for founders and executives who need a senior partner, not a vendor
- **Enterprise Architecture & Modernization** — legacy systems, migration 
  strategy, and platform stabilization at scale
- **MVP Rescue & Full-Stack Delivery** — auditing, stabilizing, and 
  shipping production-ready systems fast
- **AI/LLM Integration** — embedding AI capabilities where they create 
  real leverage, with governance and cost controls built in
- **Small Business Technical Ownership** — full technical ownership on 
  retainer for businesses that need a CTO without the full-time cost
